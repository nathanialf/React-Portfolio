## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

_**In Short:**_ _We do not collect any personal information._

Syndicate does not require an account, and it does not include analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services. We do not collect, receive, or store any personal information about you.

**Information kept on your device.** The feeds you subscribe to, the articles downloaded from them, your read and saved status, and your app settings are stored only on your device. This information is never sent to us.

**Connections to feed publishers.** To refresh your feeds, Syndicate connects directly to the websites you subscribe to and downloads their feeds, images, and site icons. Embedded content, such as YouTube videos, is loaded from the service that hosts it. As with any web request, those websites and services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address. We do not receive this information, and its use is governed by each publisher's own privacy policy.

**Application Data.** Syndicate may request the following permissions, which you can change at any time in your device's settings:

*   _Notifications._ To let you know when new articles are available in feeds you have chosen to be notified about.
    
*   _Network access._ To download the feeds you subscribe to.
    

OPML import and export only read or write the file you choose, and that file is never sent to us.

## 2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

_**In Short:**_ _We do not process your personal information._

All of Syndicate's features run on your device. We do not operate any servers that receive information from the app.

## 5. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

Because we do not collect your information, it cannot be exposed through us. Data stored by Syndicate is protected by your device's own security, so we recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

We do not knowingly collect, solicit data from, or market to children under 18 years of age or the equivalent age as specified by law in your jurisdiction.

## 8. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

_**In Short:**_ _You keep full control of your information because it stays on your device._

In some regions (like the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and Canada), and in some US states, you have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. Because we do not hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You can manage or remove all of Syndicate's data directly on your device at any time.

If you have questions or comments about your privacy rights, you may email us at [privacy@defnf.com](mailto:privacy@defnf.com).
